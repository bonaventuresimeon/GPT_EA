# GPT_EA Jurisdiction-Ready Legal Review Checklist

Use this checklist before selling, licensing, advertising, distributing or supporting GPT_EA in a new country, state, province or other legal jurisdiction.

This is a legal-review framework, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Complete it with qualified counsel for each target market.

## 2. Product classification

Counsel should determine whether GPT_EA is treated locally as general software, trading software, automated trading software, investment research/tooling, financial promotion, investment advice, portfolio management, signal service, algorithmic trading service, regulated financial service, consumer digital content, or another regulated classification.

Do not assume the classification is the same in every jurisdiction. Review whether human approval, user-owned broker accounts and user-controlled risk settings change the local classification.

## 3. Licensing / regulatory perimeter

- [ ] Whether the seller needs a financial-services license/registration.
- [ ] Whether software-only distribution is outside the local regulated perimeter.
- [ ] Whether providing trade signals, recommendations or personalization changes that result.
- [ ] Whether automated execution triggers additional licensing duties.
- [ ] Whether marketing performance claims are regulated.
- [ ] Whether affiliates/influencers require disclosures.
- [ ] Whether cross-border solicitation is restricted.
- [ ] Whether local broker/derivatives restrictions affect customer eligibility.
- [ ] Whether professional/retail distinctions matter.
- [ ] Whether any jurisdiction should be blocked from purchase/use.

Record counsel conclusion and supporting authority/reference.

## 4. Trading-risk disclosure

Confirm the local disclosure adequately explains leveraged trading can cause substantial loss; loss of all allocated capital is possible; slippage/gaps/broker execution can exceed expected loss; AI/automation can be wrong or unavailable; historical results do not guarantee future results; no wealth/profit/fixed-return guarantee exists; and users remain responsible for funding, leverage, broker, settings and approvals.

Check whether mandatory regulator-specific risk wording is required.

## 5. Consumer-protection review

- [ ] Mandatory cancellation/cooling-off rights.
- [ ] Digital-content waiver/consent requirements.
- [ ] Refund rules.
- [ ] Unfair-contract-term restrictions.
- [ ] Mandatory warranties.
- [ ] Fitness/merchantability rules.
- [ ] Chargeback/consumer-remedy rules.
- [ ] Pre-contract disclosure requirements.
- [ ] Language/localization requirements.
- [ ] Font/prominence requirements for risk warnings.

Any clause that attempts to waive mandatory consumer rights must be removed or qualified.

## 6. Liability clauses

Review limitation of liability, exclusion of indirect/consequential damages, ordinary market-loss allocation, liability cap, indemnity, force majeure and third-party service failures.

Counsel must identify liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded. Do not state that the owner can never be sued, summoned, investigated or required to appear before a competent court/regulator.

## 8. Intellectual property / anti-piracy

Confirm local enforceability of copyright ownership, license restrictions, no redistribution/resale, no sublicensing, account/license limits, anti-circumvention clauses, reverse-engineering limits, source-code confidentiality, trademark/branding protection and takedown/enforcement procedures.

Reverse-engineering restrictions must include any mandatory-law exception.

## 9. License suspension / revocation

Review piracy, chargeback fraud, credential sharing, unauthorized redistribution, tampered/cracked builds, circumvention and material breach. Confirm notice/cure requirements and consumer rights.

License failure must block new entries safely and must not intentionally endanger already-open positions.

## 10. Privacy / data protection

Document every personal-data field processed by purchase/license systems, support, licensing, analytics/telemetry and crash logs. Prefer privacy-minimized identifiers.

Review privacy notice, lawful basis/consent, retention, cross-border transfers, processor agreements, access/deletion rights, security obligations and breach notification.

Never collect MT5 passwords or customer OpenAI API keys merely for licensing.

## 11. OpenAI / third-party terms

Confirm documentation accurately separates ChatGPT subscription, OpenAI API account/billing, customer-owned API key, MetaTrader/broker relationship and VPS/internet services. Do not imply endorsement or affiliation that does not exist.

## 12. Marketing / financial promotions

Review every claim concerning accuracy, confidence, returns, win rate, drawdown, historical results, AI intelligence, passive income, wealth, guaranteed, safe and risk-free.

Prohibit unsupported or misleading claims. Distinguish backtest, demo and live results and state period, assumptions and limitations.

## 13. Tax / invoicing

Review VAT/GST/sales tax, digital-services tax, invoice requirements, withholding, marketplace obligations and local currency/payment restrictions.

## 14. Customer acknowledgement flow

Counsel should review CUSTOMER_RISK_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_FLOW.md and the current in-EA acknowledgement wording.

Confirm acknowledgements are conspicuous, affirmative and not pre-ticked; exact terms version is recorded; customer can access terms before accepting; evidence retention is lawful; material terms changes force re-acceptance where required; and acceptance does not purport to waive mandatory rights.

## 15. Support / complaint process

Review CUSTOMER_SUPPORT_RUNBOOK.md and confirm complaint contacts, response commitments, escalation, regulator/ombudsman rights where mandatory, records retention, and language/accessibility obligations.
